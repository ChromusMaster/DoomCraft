# Project Policy

## Free project

DoomCraft is provided without charge.

- no purchase is required;
- no subscription is required;
- no donation is required;
- no sponsorship is required;
- no feature is intentionally locked behind payment;
- payment does not provide priority support or exclusive builds.

Testing, feedback, documentation improvements, and responsible bug reports are sufficient contributions.

## Suggestions

Constructive suggestions are welcome.

No suggestion is automatically promised for implementation. Requests are evaluated according to:

- technical feasibility;
- stability;
- accessibility;
- platform compatibility;
- performance;
- maintenance burden;
- legal and licensing requirements;
- impact on existing worlds and saves.

Submit suggestions at:

https://github.com/ChromusMaster/DoomCraft/issues

## Modpack permission

DoomCraft may be included in public or private modpacks when:

1. the mod is identified as DoomCraft;
2. Chromus Master is credited as the project author;
3. the original CurseForge project is referenced;
4. the mod is not presented as created or officially maintained by the modpack author;
5. the standalone JAR is not extracted from the pack and offered as an independent mirror;
6. WADs and third-party content comply with their own licenses.

A modpack must not distribute commercial or otherwise restricted WADs without permission.

## Official binary distribution

The project designates CurseForge as the official distribution channel for public DoomCraft JAR releases.

Please do not mirror or re-upload the standalone JAR to:

- Modrinth;
- personal sites;
- forums;
- Discord file attachments;
- cloud drives;
- file-hosting services;
- advertisement or link-shortener pages;
- paid download services.

Source code, patches, build scripts, and license notices remain available in the public GitHub repository.

DoomCraft is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. Nothing on this page replaces the rights and obligations established by the applicable software licenses.

## WAD policy

DoomCraft does not include WADs.

Users and modpack maintainers are responsible for confirming that any WAD they use or distribute is legally permitted.

## Conduct

When participating in issues:

- provide reproducible technical information;
- avoid harassment;
- do not demand support deadlines;
- do not post pirated content;
- do not publish private user data;
- keep platform and mod compatibility discussions factual.
